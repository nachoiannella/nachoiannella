## Context

Most travel apps are built for the moment of *buying*. But the trip itself, the part people actually care about, happens after, often far from home and sometimes without a connection.

Despegar’s own UX research pointed the same way: managing bookings is the main reason people use a travel app, most travelers use the Despegar app during their trip, and part of the people who open it at the destination do so offline.

## My role

I designed **Travel Mode** (*Modo Viaje*) **from scratch, from ideation to production**, together with my content-design partner (UX writer), as a Sr. UX/UI Designer at Despegar.

## The problem

When you land in a new city you need three things fast: **your bookings, proof that you have them, and what to do next.** An app organized around searching and buying doesn’t give you that, and if the connection fails, the most important thing (the voucher) can be out of reach.

## Key decisions

<Decision title="A mode that turns on by itself" chose="An experience that activates automatically when the trip dates arrive" over="Another section buried in “My trips”">
Context changes what matters, so the app changes with it, without the traveler having to look for it.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Works without a connection" chose="Bookings and vouchers available without a connection" over="Assuming connectivity and showing an error state">
The worst moment for a spinner is at a hotel desk or a border. Travel Mode became **the first Despegar app feature to work offline**.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Context that earns its place" chose="Nearby places, events, destination info and offers on cars and activities" over="A generic content feed">
The bar for showing anything during a trip is: is it useful *here and now*?
</Decision>

## Outcome

- Shipped in the Despegar app: **the first feature that works without a connection**, activating on its own when the trip begins.
- A **100% native solution, new for Despegar**, designed from zero.
- It turned the app from a place to buy into a companion during the trip, with bookings, vouchers, nearby places, events and offers at hand.
