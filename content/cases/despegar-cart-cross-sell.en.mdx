## Context

Despegar is Latin America’s leading travel-tech company, with more than 30 million customers. For most of its history, a trip was bought the way the catalog was organized: a flight here, a hotel there, a package if that exact combination existed. Each product had its own funnel.

In December 2025 Despegar launched its **shopping cart**, on web and app: one place to combine flights, hotels (even several, on different dates), cars, transfers and activities, and pay for everything in a single purchase — with extra discounts for buying together. Despegar presents it as the first online tool of its kind in the travel industry. It’s integrated with SOFIA, the company’s generative-AI assistant, and in 2026 it launched in Brazil as Decolar’s *Carrinho de Compras*.

## My role

I returned to Despegar in **September 2025 as UX Manager**, leading a team of **3 designers** (2 senior, 1 semi-senior). Together with the **Cart & Cross-sell squad** — product and engineering — we carry the vision of the shopping cart and cross-selling at Despegar.

When I arrived, the cart was at a **very early stage**. My contribution was a different perspective: new ways of doing things, and thinking outside the box about what buying a whole trip online could be.

## The problem

Selling a *trip* instead of *products* sounds obvious until you design it:

- **More in the cart, without a heavier checkout.** Every product type brings its own rules — dates, passengers, conditions, payment options — and they all have to fit in one clear purchase.
- **Cross-sell that helps instead of nags.** A transfer or a car is useful to someone who just booked a flight and a hotel — when it shows up as part of their trip, not as an interruption.
- **Savings people understand.** “Buy together and save” only works if the saving is clear.

## How we work

This is joint work: design, product and engineering in the same squad, shaping one vision instead of handing off screens. As I wrote when the cart launched: it’s a cart unique in the industry, and we’re excited to keep building and improving it with the help and feedback of our users.

## Outcome

- Launched in **December 2025** on web and app, then in **Brazil (Decolar)** in 2026.
- Presented by Despegar as the **first online tool of its kind** in the travel industry, integrated with SOFIA.
- And it keeps evolving: the squad keeps iterating on the cart and cross-sell with what we learn from travelers.
