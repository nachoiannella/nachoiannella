## Context

In March 2024 Despegar launched **SOFIA**, which it describes as the first travel assistant in Latin America to combine generative AI with an *incremental and adaptive visual interface*. SOFIA accompanies travelers from the moment the idea of a trip appears — through planning and coordination — until they come back home.

It’s multimodal: people can write, talk or share images, and it’s available any time, across the region. Under the hood, Despegar built it on GPT-4, OpenAI’s Whisper and AWS.

## My role

When I joined Despegar as a Sr. UX/UI Designer, **SOFIA didn’t exist**. We designed the SOFIA platform **from scratch**: the conversation, the cards, the voice — everything. As a teammate put it publicly when SOFIA turned one: UX was there from the very beginning.

## The problem

Travel planning is visual, comparative and full of second thoughts. A text box and a wall of text wasn’t going to be enough.

The challenge was to make something that feels like a **real travel agent**: one that starts from a vague desire (“I want to go surfing”), adapts to how you react, and ends in concrete recommendations — without forcing a fixed order of questions.

## How we worked

New technology, no playbook. A teammate summed up the spirit of that first year: the importance of just *going for it*, even when we didn’t know exactly what or how — and learning on the way everything the technology had to offer.

<Decision title="Not a chatbot: a conversation you can see" chose="A hybrid interface where the conversation builds visual options — destinations, dates, prices — you can tap" over="A pure chat window">
People can drop the chat entirely and just touch visual elements or talk.

<Decision title="Meet people in their mode" chose="Voice, text and images as inputs" over="Text only">
A trip idea can start with a photo or a voice note.
</Decision>

<Decision title="No script" chose="A non-linear conversation that adapts to how the traveler reacts" over="A decision tree with a fixed order of questions">
That’s what makes it feel like an agent, not a form.
</Decision>

<Decision title="Know when to hand off" chose="A path to self-serve purchase, or to a person from Despegar for sales or after-sales" over="Keeping people inside the AI at all costs">
Trust grows when the assistant knows its limits.
</Decision>

## Outcome

- Launched across Latin America on **4 March 2024**, covered by Business Wire, PR Newswire, Forbes Chile and Forbes Perú.
- Turned one in 2025, and in December 2025 became part of the purchase itself, integrated into the shopping cart.

> When SOFIA turned one, I admitted publicly: “I won’t deny I miss Sofi.”

## What I learned

- **Bringing LLMs into an everyday product.** Not a demo or a lab: a feature real travelers use to plan and buy.
- **Using new technology to solve what the traditional site couldn’t** — starting from an idea instead of a search form, and adapting to each person.
